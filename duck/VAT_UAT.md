# VAT / UAT
## Founder acceptance test
1. Open deployed public link on phone.
2. Understand it in under five seconds.
3. Click Book a time without AI.
4. Pick a real free slot.
5. Complete booking.
6. Observe host event and visitor invitation.
7. Cancel/reschedule and observe provider state.
8. Break AI credentials deliberately.
9. Repeat direct booking successfully.
10. Submit an intent and compare two configured AI models.

Private pilot requires all above `VERIFIED` plus security/abuse tests and provider evidence.
